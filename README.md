# Digital Forensics Research Portfolio

A responsive, single-page interactive website. Each of the five research modules expands inline on the same page.

## Open the portfolio
Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build step, package manager or server is required.

## Included sections
- Digital Forensics fundamentals, objectives, evidence categories, workflow, chain of custody and integrity.
- Image & Video Forensics.
- Digital Forensics Engineer career research.
- Free Mind-Mapping Tools comparison.
- MBR & Disk Forensics, including BIOS/UEFI, GPT, boot sequence, encryption and safe forensic workflow.
- 26 July Axis digital billboard incident case study, with public timeline, source-confidence labels, architecture diagrams, evidence matrix and security recommendations.
- Source register with direct references.

## Important research limitations
- The billboard case study separates the official public account, company/operator statements, media reporting and attacker claims. Public reporting does not establish the attackers' verified identities or nationality, exact exploit/CVE, firewall product, complete affected-country list or final forensic root cause.
- Job-market and free-plan information changes. Before academic submission, update the employer/vacancy dataset and verify current free-plan restrictions from official pages, recording the date checked.
- The MBR exercise is designed for disposable virtual disks and forensic copies only. Do not modify original or third-party evidence.
